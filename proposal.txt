Project Overview:
My project creates a restaurant order processing simulation to show how customer 
orders move through different food cuisines over some time. The simulation shows 
how the customers’ orders change from waiting to cooking to complete when dining 
time is busy. Then, it will following each order from being accepted to being 
cooked to being finished.

Implementation:
I plan to use a std::map where the key is the kitchen name such as americanKitchen, 
mexicanKitchen, and italianKitchen. The map’s value will be a three element 
std::array of std::lists that store orders in the different kitchens. It will start 
with list 0 where the orders are waiting to be started, then list 1 where the orders 
are currently being cooked, and lastly list 2 where the orders are completed and ready 
to serve.
