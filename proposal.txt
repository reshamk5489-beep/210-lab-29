Project Overview:
My project creates a restaurant order processing simulation to show how customer orders 
move through different food cuisines over some time. The simulation shows how the 
customers’ orders change from waiting period to cooking period to completion period.

Implementation:
I plan to use a std::map where the key is the cuisine name such as americanCuisine, 
mexicanCuisine, and italianCuisine. The map’s value will be an std::array of three 
elements each containing std::lists of orders. The first element of the array stored the 
orders that are waiting to be started, then the second will store orders that are currently 
being cooked, and lastly the third will store orders that are completed and ready to serve.

Simulated events:
The simulation will focus on three main events: the new order that is waiting, then the 
cooking phase, and lastly the completed phase. At any given time period, new orders may enter 
the system, existing orders may move from waiting to cooking, and finished meals may move to 
the completed stage.
